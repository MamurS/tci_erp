**I do not see a section a colleague sees.**
Sections follow the acting role. Check the workspace at the top of the menu; if you do not hold the role you need, the administrator grants it.

**Why does a screen say "no access"?**
The acting role does not open it. The system offers to open it in another role, if you hold one.

**Why can I not close a task in the Agenda?**
Tasks close by themselves when the work is done. Open the task with its main button — it takes you to the screen where the task is done.

**I switched the language — some data stayed in another language.**
The interface is fully translated; company names, comments and documents stay in the language they were entered in.

**Where do I see who changed a decision and when?**
Decisions, status history and events never change — a new one supersedes the old, and the whole chain is shown on the object's page.

**An amount shows as "—".**
The value is unknown (for example, there is no exchange rate). An empty value is never shown as zero.

**What is demo mode?**
Training data whose names start with DEMO; switched on in the user menu, see {{link:start-demo}}.

**"A new version of TCI ERP is available" appeared.**
The system was updated. Save what you typed and press the reload button in the notice.
