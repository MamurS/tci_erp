Help opens three ways:

- the «?» button in the top bar or the <kbd>F1</kbd> key — the topic about the open page, the one written for your acting role first;
- the small «?» next to the page title — the same;
- {{ui:nav.help}} in the menu — the Help home.

## The Help home

Here: search over every topic, the contents (your role's sections first), the glossary, questions and answers, a "Quick start" PDF for each role and the {{ui:help.replayTour}} button.

## Searching the guide

Every word of the query must appear — in titles or text. Case, the letters "ё" and "е" and Uzbek Latin apostrophes do not matter: `so‘rov`, `so'rov` and `sorov` find the same. The words found are marked in the topic.
