{{ui:agenda.title}} is the work queue. **The system creates the tasks**: when a submission moves to your department, when a limit review falls due, when a policyholder files a declaration. When the work is done — the decision recorded, the rating confirmed — the task closes by itself.

{{shot:start-agenda}}

## Two views

- **{{ui:agenda.views.list}}** — one row per piece of work, grouped by due date, company or type; task-family chips with counts.
- **{{ui:agenda.views.triage}}** — the queue on the left, the selected task on the right: facts, comments, history. The main button takes you to the screen where the task is done (for example, the limit decision form).

## What you can do with a task

- **{{ui:agenda.snooze.action}}** — the task hides until the chosen moment and comes back by itself.
- **{{ui:agenda.transfer.action}}** — one task or many go to an active colleague of the same department, with an optional note. Limit validation steps and rating proposals are never handed on: who answers them is decided by authority.
- **Comment** — stays in the task's history for good.

Keys: <kbd>J</kbd>/<kbd>K</kbd> — down/up, <kbd>Enter</kbd> or <kbd>O</kbd> — open, <kbd>S</kbd> — snooze, <kbd>C</kbd> — comment.

> [!NOTE]
> A task can almost never be closed "by hand": it closes when its reason goes away. The "Done" button exists only on notices after which nothing happens automatically.
