Display settings are personal and kept on the server: on any computer you see the system as you set it up. They are in the user menu ({{ui:account.display.title}}) and on {{ui:account.title}}.

- **Zoom** — 100–200 %, enlarges the whole interface, like browser zoom. The default is 150 %.
- **Text size** — 100–150 %, enlarges only the text; spacing and layout stay as they are.
- **Colour theme** — blue (the default), salad green, teal, plum, graphite; light, dark or "as the device".
- **Text on coloured badges** — pastel badges with dark text (the default) or solid ones with white.

Printed documents — the risk report, a draft contract, a commercial offer — always print at 100 % and in the house colours, whatever you chose.
