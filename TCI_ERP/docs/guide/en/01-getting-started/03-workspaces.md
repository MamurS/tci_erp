A person may hold several roles — say, credit analyst and credit underwriter. But they work **in one role at a time**: this is the {{term:acting-role|acting role}}, and every rule in the database answers for it. Roles do not add up.

## How to switch roles

1. Open the {{ui:nav.workspace}} switcher at the top of the menu.
2. Pick a workspace: {{ui:nav.workspaces.commercial.name}}, {{ui:nav.workspaces.credit.name}}, {{ui:nav.workspaces.analysis.name}}, {{ui:nav.workspaces.sales.name}}, {{ui:nav.workspaces.claims.name}}, {{ui:nav.workspaces.data.name}} or {{ui:nav.workspaces.admin.name}}.
3. The system tells the server the new role, refreshes the data and opens the workspace's first tool.

The acting role is shown in the user menu, top right. The choice is remembered in the browser and offered at your next sign-in.

## What changes with the role

- **Menu and screens** — only what this role opens.
- **Agenda** — the tasks of this role's department and the tasks addressed to you personally as a member of that department.
- **Buttons** — for example, a credit decision is available only while acting as a credit underwriter.

> [!NOTE]
> If you open a link to a screen the acting role does not have, the system shows "no access" and offers to open it in another role you hold. It never switches the role by itself.
