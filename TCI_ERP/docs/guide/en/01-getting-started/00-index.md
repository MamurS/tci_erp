TCI ERP is the trade credit insurance system: the company registry, credit underwriting of buyers, policies and their terms, turnover declarations, premium, overdue notifications and claims. This section is for everyone on staff: how to sign in, how the screen is laid out, how workspaces and the acting role work, where to search and how to read your {{term:agenda|Agenda}}.
