There is **one search field** — in the top bar. It searches the whole system and filters the list open on the page at the same time.

## How to search

1. Press <kbd>Ctrl</kbd>/<kbd>⌘</kbd> + <kbd>K</kbd> or click the search field.
2. Type a company name, a policy, claim or submission number, a registration number or a country.
3. Separate several terms with commas — they apply in order, each narrowing what the previous left: "Samarkand, TCI-2026, UZ".
4. Pick a result from the list, or press <kbd>Enter</kbd> to filter the open page.

## Rules

- A term that **exactly** equals a value (a policy number, a registration number) finds exactly that: "TCI-10000" does not find TCI-100001.
- Case, accents, extra spaces and the different apostrophes of Uzbek Latin do not matter.
- The query is kept in the page address — send a filtered list to a colleague as a link.
- The <kbd>Ctrl</kbd>/<kbd>⌘</kbd> + <kbd>K</kbd> palette also offers the acting role's actions, for example {{ui:palette.actions.requestCompany}}.

> [!TIP]
> Company not found? Ask for it to be registered: {{ui:data.notFound.request}} — the request goes to an information manager (see {{link:data-requests}}).
