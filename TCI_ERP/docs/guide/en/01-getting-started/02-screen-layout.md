## The menu on the left

The menu is grouped by **workspace**. At the top is the workspace switcher, below it "Shared" ({{ui:nav.dashboard}}, {{ui:nav.agenda}}, {{ui:nav.entities}}, {{ui:nav.sanctions}}, {{ui:nav.help}}), and below that your workspace's own tools. Pin or hide the menu with the button in the top left corner or with <kbd>Ctrl</kbd>/<kbd>⌘</kbd> + <kbd>B</kbd>. A hidden menu appears when you rest the pointer at the left edge of the window.

## The top bar

On the left, the name of the open section. In the middle, **the one search field** (see {{link:start-search}}). On the right, the «?» button (help for the open page; <kbd>F1</kbd> does the same), the RU / EN / UZ language switcher and the user menu: the acting role, display settings, {{ui:account.title}} and sign-out.

## The dashboard

The dashboard shows work, not a showcase: a strip of key figures (each leads to its screen), your Agenda tasks — the most urgent first — and the signals that are not zero right now.

> [!TIP]
> Next to the title of almost every page there is a small «?» — it opens the help topic about that very page.
