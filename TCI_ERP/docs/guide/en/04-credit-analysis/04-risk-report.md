## When to use it

To show a buyer's assessment to colleagues or the committee, or to print it.

## Steps

1. The {{ui:entities.tabs.overview}} tab of the card is the online risk report.
2. To print, open the A4 report: choose the language (Russian, English, Uzbek), the statement type and, if needed, a currency to convert to.
3. Save to PDF with the browser's print.

## Result

The report: the rating scale, strengths and weaknesses, key figures, the group's statements, ratios, commentary, a glossary, the group structure. Pages are numbered "page X of Y".

## Common mistakes

- **Reading the group total as a consolidation.** The group figures are a plain sum of the members' statements with no intra-group eliminations; the report says so under the table.
