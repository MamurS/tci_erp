## When to use it

A task in the Agenda about a {{term:possible-match|possible match}}: a company, a company above it in the group or a beneficial owner resembles a sanctions list entry.

## Steps

1. Open the task or {{ui:nav.sanctions}} › matches.
2. Compare the company's or person's data with the list entry: full name, country, dates, identifiers.
3. **Confirm** the match — the company gets a sanctions flag, and an urgent review task opens for each of its active limits.
4. Or **clear** the match with a note. A cleared match is never raised again for the same name and the same entry.

## Result

While a match waits for review, no auto decision on the buyer is possible and an approving credit decision is refused.

## Common mistakes

- **Expecting limits to be revoked automatically.** A flag revokes nothing by itself: it opens review tasks, and credit underwriting decides.
