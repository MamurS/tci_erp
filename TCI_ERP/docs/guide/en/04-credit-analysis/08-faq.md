**Why do I not see the decision queue?**
A credit analyst does not decide limits: in {{ui:nav.limits}} the ratings, monitoring, register and groups are open to them.

**Can I confirm a grade with a different grade?**
Yes, but only with a comment. A grade is confirmed once, and only the buyer's current one.

**Why did my proposal go to the next rank?**
A grade has no amount, so escalation goes rank by rank, starting above yours, skipping ranks where nobody can answer.

**Do I need authority to set an ADT?**
Yes: an analyst needs authority grants of their own (the administrator gives them). Limit decisions are still never routed to an analyst.
