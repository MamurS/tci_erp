The credit analyst assesses buyers: enters and analyses financial statements, calculates and confirms the {{term:grade|grade}}, proposes a {{term:manual-rating|manual rating}}, reviews sanctions matches, keeps the sector grades and sets the {{term:adt|ADT}} within their own authority. A senior credit analyst sets the {{term:group-rating|group rating}}.

The credit analyst **never decides a limit**. Their workspace is {{ui:nav.workspaces.analysis.name}}.

> [!NOTE]
> Who rates depends on the credit analysis mode the administrator sets: "combined" (the default: analysts and credit underwriters both rate) or "separate" (only analysts rate and confirm; underwriters only decide).
