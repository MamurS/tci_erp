## When to use it

Every day.

## Steps

1. {{ui:nav.agenda}}: a buyer with no grade, a stale grade, new statements to rate, a colleague's rating proposal, a possible sanctions match, the quarterly sector review, an ADT review.
2. {{ui:nav.limits}} › {{ui:limits.views.ratings}}: the rating worklist with the reason — no statements, no grade, new statements, a grade older than 12 months, an unconfirmed grade.

{{shot:analysis-ratings}}

3. Open the company, tab {{ui:entities.tabs.rating}}: calculate the grade, confirm it or propose your own (see {{link:analysis-rating}}).
4. {{ui:nav.sanctions}}: review the possible matches assigned to you.
5. {{ui:nav.sectors}}: review your sectors when a new quarter starts.

## Result

The grades a decision waits for are confirmed; the matches are reviewed.

## Common mistakes

- **Confirming a grade you calculated yourself.** That is the four-eyes rule: one calculates, another confirms. To approve your own assessment, use the "approve myself" route in the rating form.
