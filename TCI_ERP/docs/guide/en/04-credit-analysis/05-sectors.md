## When to use it

Your sector's quarterly review (a task in the Agenda, due on the 15th of the quarter's first month) or an event out of turn.

## Steps

1. {{ui:nav.sectors}}: the sector grades A–D with their outlook, and the OECD country risk categories.
2. Open your sector and create a review: the grade, the outlook, the seven factors (favourable / neutral / adverse, with a note), the narrative and the sources.
3. When four eyes are on, the review comes into force once another analyst approves it.
4. An OECD country category may be overridden by an internal one — with a justification; a senior credit analyst approves it.

## Result

The sector grade and the country category in force feed the {{term:cover-matrix|cover matrix}}: the worse the country and the sector, the higher the rank that decides a limit. A sector downgraded to C or D tells credit underwriting about the buyers with active limits.

## Common mistakes

- **Expecting an old grade to stay current.** A grade from last quarter is marked stale, and without a review after the 15th, overdue.
