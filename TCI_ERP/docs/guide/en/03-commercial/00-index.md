title: Commercial underwriting
roles: [commercial_underwriter]
---

The commercial underwriter owns the **policy terms**: the premium rate, the {{term:insured-percentage|insured percentage}}, the {{term:nql|non-qualifying loss (NQL)}}, the {{term:deductible|deductible}}, the {{term:afl|aggregate first loss}}, the {{term:max-liability|maximum liability}}, the {{term:dl|discretionary limit}}, the waiting period and the maximum payment terms. They also run the **commercial stage** of limit decisions, draft contracts and turnover declarations.

The commercial underwriter never changes the grade or the conditions of a credit decision — only the amount and the payment terms, within their own authority.
