## When to use it

A credit underwriter has decided and a commercial-stage task has appeared in your Agenda.

## Steps

1. Open the limit request.
2. If the amount or the payment terms should differ from the credit decision, adjust them. The grade and the conditions of the credit decision cannot be changed.
3. An increase is checked against your commercial authority in the buyer's rating band and against the group limit; above your authority — {{ui:limits.validation.send}}.
4. A reduction is not limited by authority and reaches the client at once.

## Result

After the commercial stage the decision goes to sales and reaches the client when the sales window ends.

## Common mistakes

- **Adjusting your own request.** Whoever raised a request does not adjust it; whoever made the credit decision does not run its commercial stage.
