## When to use it

Every day, starting from the Agenda.

## Steps

1. {{ui:nav.agenda}}: submissions in commercial review, credit decisions at the commercial stage, draft contracts under review, accepted declarations with uncovered excess, renewals and reviews.
2. {{ui:nav.policies}} › {{ui:policies.views.overview}}: the policy book — premium, loss ratio, policies due for renewal.
3. Submissions in {{ui:requests.statuses.commercial_review}}: set the terms and pass them to sales.
4. {{ui:nav.declarations}}: accept or dispute the declarations filed.

## Result

Terms set, declarations accepted, contracts agreed.

## Common mistakes

- **Adjusting a limit you decided on the credit stage yourself.** Whoever made the credit decision does not run its commercial stage.
