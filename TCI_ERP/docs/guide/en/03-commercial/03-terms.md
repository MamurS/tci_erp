## Structure

- **Whole turnover** — every buyer of the policyholder is covered (within the limits and the discretionary limit).
- **Key buyers** — only the named buyers are covered.

## Terms

| Term | What it means |
|---|---|
| {{term:insured-percentage|Insured percentage}} | The share of the loss the insurer pays, usually 80–90 %. |
| {{term:nql|Non-qualifying loss (NQL)}} | A threshold: a loss below it is not paid at all; one equal to it is paid in full, with nothing deducted. |
| {{term:deductible|Deductible}} | An amount taken off each loss after the insured percentage. |
| {{term:afl|Aggregate first loss}} | Losses in a period the policyholder bears themselves; what is left of it is deducted. |
| {{term:max-liability|Maximum liability}} | The cap on all payments under the policy, usually a multiple of the premium. |
| {{term:dl|Discretionary limit}} | Up to this amount the policyholder assesses a buyer themselves, with no limit. |
| Waiting period | How many days after the overdue date a claim may be filed. |
| Maximum payment terms | Invoices with longer terms are not covered. |

## The order of the indemnity

Covered debt → NQL threshold → × insured percentage → − deductible → − what is left of the aggregate first loss → no more than what is left of the maximum liability. In detail: {{link:claims-coverage}}.
