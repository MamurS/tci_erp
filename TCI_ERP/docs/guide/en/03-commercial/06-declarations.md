## When to use it

A policyholder has filed a {{term:turnover-declaration|turnover declaration}} — or premium is due.

## Steps

1. {{ui:nav.declarations}}: open the filed declaration.
2. Check the lines per buyer. The system splits the turnover: for a buyer with an active limit the whole turnover is covered; without a limit only up to the discretionary limit, and the rest is **uncovered excess** (not insured, and shown separately for commercial review).
3. Accept the declaration or dispute it. On acceptance the split is frozen and premium is earned on the covered turnover at the policy's rate.
4. The policy's premium tab: the instalment schedule, invoices, payments. A payment fills the earliest instalments first; an overpayment is refused; a mistake is corrected by a reversal, never an edit.

## Result

An accepted declaration never changes; a correction is a new declaration that supersedes it.

## Common mistakes

- **Expecting a refund below the minimum premium.** There is no refund below the minimum premium.
