**Why can I not increase a limit at the commercial stage?**
An increase above your commercial authority or beyond the group limit needs validation. A reduction is always possible.

**Where do contract templates come from?**
The administrator uploads them: {{ui:nav.policies}} › {{ui:policies.views.templates}}. One active template per country, language and product.

**Why does a policy have no premium schedule?**
It was created before schedules existed. Create the schedule on the premium tab.

**Who sees the {{term:policyholder-tier|policyholder tier}}?**
Staff only. A tier is a service class; it never affects limits, premium or authority.
