**Why do I see the departments' screens but cannot change anything?**
The administrator sees the business screens for oversight only. Every business action is closed in the database.

**How do I unlock a user after failed sign-ins?**
{{ui:admin.tabs.security}} › lockouts › unlock. Unlocking needs the lockout to be active.

**Where is the second factor switched on?**
Each person connects it on {{ui:account.title}}; for the administrator it is mandatory.
