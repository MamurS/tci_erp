## When to use it

A new colleague, a change of duties, a forgotten password, a lockout.

## Steps

1. {{ui:nav.admin}} › {{ui:admin.tabs.users}}.

{{shot:admin-users}}

2. Roles: tick one or several. The client role is never combined with another. A user works in one role at a time and switches through the workspaces.
3. A credit analyst may be marked "senior" — they set group ratings and approve internal country categories.
4. The person's manager, ban and unban, password reset (the user changes the temporary password at sign-in).
5. Users are created through the user-provisioning service. Until it is hosted, the screen says "service unavailable" and accounts are created by hand following the system owner's instructions.

## Result

The user sees the workspaces of their roles.

## Common mistakes

- **Giving yourself a business role to "fix" something.** Business actions belong to the departments: that is what keeps the four-eyes rule.
