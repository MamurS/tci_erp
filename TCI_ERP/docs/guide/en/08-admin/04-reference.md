## {{ui:admin.tabs.risk}}

- The OECD list: the OECD's PDF is loaded as published; before the import the screen shows which countries change category and which buyers with active limits it affects. Once the list is older than 4 months, a reminder appears in the Agenda.
- Sanctions lists: credit analysts and the administrator import them; a daily update runs automatically.
- Rating model adjustments by sector and country, the concentration threshold.

## {{ui:admin.tabs.tiers}}

The thresholds of the bronze, silver, gold and platinum tiers by insured turnover and premium.

## Contract templates

{{ui:nav.policies}} › {{ui:policies.views.templates}}: .docx files with placeholders, per country, language and product; one active version per key; a template is never edited — a new version is uploaded.

## Demo data

{{ui:nav.admin}} › {{ui:admin.tabs.demo}}: load or remove the training set. Demo records start with "DEMO" and are seen by those who switched on demo mode in the user menu.
