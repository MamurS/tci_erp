## When to use it

Daily and when colleagues ask.

## Steps

1. {{ui:nav.agenda}}: your own tasks — the reminder to update the OECD list and other technical ones.
2. {{ui:nav.admin}} › {{ui:admin.tabs.security}}: sign-in lockouts, alerts, sessions.
3. Colleagues' requests: new users, roles, password resets, unlocking.
4. Exchange rates and reference data as needed.

## Result

Everyone works in their roles; no account is locked without a reason.

## Common mistakes

- **Trying to "help" with a limit decision.** The administrator makes no decisions — the database refuses.
