## When to use it

A colleague found nothing in the search and pressed {{ui:data.notFound.request}}, or pressed {{ui:data.statementsRequest.button}} on a company card.

## Steps

1. Anyone on staff files the request: the name, the country, what is known. If the registration number is already held, the system answers that the company exists; if the same request is already open, that it has been filed.
2. The request gets a number DR-YYYY-NNNN and goes to the **least-loaded** active information manager — into their Agenda.
3. The information manager presses {{ui:data.actions.take}} and may hand it to a colleague.
4. A company request ends **created** (the ordinary company form, duplicate check included), **linked** to an existing company, or **rejected** with a reason. A statements request ends **updated** or **rejected**.
5. Whoever asked is told the result.

## Result

The company is in the registry, and whoever asked knows it.

## Common mistakes

- **Expecting the request to create the company by itself.** A person always saves the company through the ordinary form.
