## When to use it

A new company or a change of particulars.

## Steps

1. Fill in the country, name, registration number, address, industry, legal form, date founded, contacts.
2. The same registration number in the same country blocks saving; similar names are a hint — check them.
3. Registry lookup (GLEIF, UK Companies House) prefills the form — once the analytics service is hosted; until then, manual entry.
4. Tab {{ui:entities.tabs.sanctions}}: enter the {{term:beneficial-owner|beneficial owners}}; every change triggers a sanctions screening.
5. Tab {{ui:entities.tabs.rating}}: agency ratings (S&P, Moody's, Fitch, Ahbor-Reyting and others) — for reference; they never affect a decision.

## Result

The company is ready for a grade and limits.

## Common mistakes

- **A relationship between companies without a direction.** Hints of possible relationships (a shared email domain, address, contact, a similar name) are only hints; a person creates the relationship, stating its direction and type.
