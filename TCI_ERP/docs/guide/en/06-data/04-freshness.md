## When to use it

A limit request is waiting for data, or a company appears in the freshness list.

## Steps

1. The task "data needed for a limit request" names what is missing: the registration number, the address, the industry, statements, or statements no older than 18 months.
2. Enter what is missing. As soon as nothing is missing, the request returns to the credit underwriting queue **by itself**.
3. {{ui:data.views.freshness}}: companies with live business — an open request, an active limit, a buyer in a submission, an active policy — and their first problem.

## Result

No request waits for data.

## Common mistakes

- **Entering statements for an old period.** The period must end within the last 18 months.
