**Who gets a request when I am on leave?**
New requests are shared among the active information managers. Hand your open tasks to a colleague: {{ui:agenda.transfer.action}}.

**A client proposed a buyer by name. What do I do?**
{{ui:data.views.proposals}}: create or find the company and link it to the proposal — the client's request becomes an ordinary limit request.

**Can I delete a company?**
No. Wrong data is corrected; history is never deleted.
