**Why do I not see the new limit I was told about?**
A limit appears after the insurer's final decision. Reductions and revocations show at once.

**Can a colleague get access?**
Yes — ask your manager at the insurer.

**Who do I ask when something is unclear?**
Your manager at the insurer. Help opens with the «?» button and the F1 key.
