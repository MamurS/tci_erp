## When to use it

The first sign-in and changing your password.

## Steps

1. Sign in with the email and the temporary password your insurer gave you.
2. Choose your own password (at least 12 characters) — the portal does not open without it.
3. Under {{ui:portal.nav.account}} you change your password, connect a second factor and set up the display.

## Result

{{ui:portal.nav.policies}} opens.

## Common mistakes

- **Forgot your password.** Contact your manager at the insurer: you will be given a temporary password.
