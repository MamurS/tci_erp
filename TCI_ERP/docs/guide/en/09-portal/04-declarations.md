## When to use it

A turnover declaration or a premium instalment is due.

## Steps

1. {{ui:portal.nav.declarations}}: enter the turnover per buyer and country for the period and file the declaration.
2. Turnover with a buyer that has a limit is covered in full; without a limit, up to the discretionary limit; the rest is not insured.
3. Once accepted, a declaration does not change: a correction is filed as a new declaration.
4. {{ui:portal.nav.premium}}: the instalment schedule, the invoices and the payments received.

## Result

Premium is earned on the accepted declaration.

## Common mistakes

- **Expecting a refund below the minimum premium.** The minimum premium is not refunded.
