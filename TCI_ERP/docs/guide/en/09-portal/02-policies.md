{{ui:portal.nav.policies}}: your policies, their status, period and terms — the insured percentage, the non-qualifying loss threshold, deductibles, the maximum payment terms, the discretionary limit.

{{shot:portal-home}}

{{ui:portal.nav.limits}}: the limits on your buyers — amount, payment terms, conditions, validity, and the history of earlier limits. A new decision appears here once the insurer has finished with it; a reduction or revocation at once.

Until a buyer has a limit, sales to them are covered only up to the policy's discretionary limit.
