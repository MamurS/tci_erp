{{ui:portal.nav.submissions}}: your submissions for insurance and how they are progressing.

{{ui:portal.nav.offers}}: the insurer's commercial offers. Open an offer, read it and answer: accept, ask for changes or decline. Accepting the offer is agreeing to the submission's terms. An offer has a validity: after it the offer cannot be accepted, but you can ask for a new one.

{{ui:portal.nav.contracts}}: the draft contracts the insurer shared with you. While a contract is under review, you can leave a comment.
