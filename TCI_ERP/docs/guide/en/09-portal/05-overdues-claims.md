## When to use it

A buyer has not paid an invoice on time.

## Steps

1. {{ui:portal.nav.overdues}}: report the overdue account — buyer, invoices, amounts, dates. Do it within the time your policy sets: a late notification can take the cover away.
2. After the notification the limit on this buyer is suspended — new shipments to them are not covered.
3. If the buyer pays — report it; the limit is not reinstated automatically, a new request is needed.
4. If the debt is still unpaid after the waiting period — {{ui:portal.nav.claims}}: file a claim with the required documents.
5. Answer the information requests — they arrive in the portal.

## Result

The claim is assessed; the decision and the payment are visible in the portal.

## Common mistakes

- **Reporting an overdue account late.** The notification deadline is in your policy; a late notification can take the cover away from the whole claim.
