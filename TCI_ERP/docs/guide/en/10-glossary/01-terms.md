### Acting role {#term-acting-role}

The role a person works in now; every rule answers for it. It changes when the workspace is switched.

### ADT {#term-adt}

Auto decision threshold: the amount up to which limit requests on a buyer are decided automatically, and how many such decisions (1–5). Set within authority; an auto decision only when every condition holds and there is no stop factor.

### Agenda {#term-agenda}

A person's task queue. The system opens and closes the tasks from events; people work them, snooze, comment on or hand them to a colleague.

### Aggregate first loss {#term-afl}

(AFL) Losses in a period the policyholder bears themselves; what is left of it after earlier claims is deducted from the indemnity.

### Annulment {#term-annulment}

The policy is treated as never concluded and the premium is returned. Not the same as cancellation.

### Authority {#term-authority}

The amount up to which a person decides alone — per stream, rating band, currency and validity. Nobody's authority is unlimited.

### Authority level {#term-authority-level}

Rank 1–4 with the level's grants and members; rank 5 is the committee. A proposed decision goes to a level, not to a named person.

### Beneficial owner {#term-beneficial-owner}

The person who ultimately owns or controls a company; screened against the sanctions lists.

### Buyer {#term-buyer}

The policyholder's debtor, whose credit risk is insured. Not our client.

### Cancellation {#term-cancellation}

The policy ends from a date; everything before it stands.

### Claim {#term-claim}

The policyholder's demand for an indemnity on a buyer's unpaid debt. Numbered CL-YYYY-NNNN.

### Commercial offer {#term-commercial-offer}

The document for the client with the terms, premium and limits of a submission. The texts are approved, the system fills in the data; an offer has a validity.

### Committee {#term-committee}

The highest, fifth authority rank. Decides by vote: quorum of half, simple majority, a tie goes the chair's way.

### Cover matrix {#term-cover-matrix}

The rule by which the OECD country category and the sector grade raise the lowest rank that may decide a limit.

### Credit limit request {#term-limit-request}

A request for a limit on a buyer under a policy or a submission. Only one may be open per policy–buyer pair; its decisions never change, a new one supersedes the old.

### Deductible {#term-deductible}

An amount taken off each loss after the insured percentage.

### Discretionary limit {#term-dl}

(DL) The amount up to which the policyholder assesses a buyer themselves, without a separate credit limit.

### Escalate {#term-escalate}

Send a proposal up the authority levels — rank by rank, up to the committee.

### Four eyes {#term-four-eyes}

The rule that the author of a request does not decide it, and whoever calculated a grade does not confirm it.

### Grade {#term-grade}

The buyer's credit risk assessment in force, on the scale A1, A2, B1, B2, C1, C2, D. In force once a person confirms it.

### Group limit {#term-group-limit}

The cap on the total exposure to a corporate group; a decision beyond it is raised to be decided with the whole group in view.

### Group rating {#term-group-rating}

A grade for a corporate group; it caps every member's grade. A senior credit analyst sets it.

### Information gathering {#term-information-gathering}

The status of a limit request whose buyer lacks data; it clears by itself once the data is entered.

### Insured percentage {#term-insured-percentage}

The share of the loss the insurer pays, usually 80–90 %.

### Limit revocation {#term-revocation}

A risk action: the limit stops covering new shipments; it reaches the policyholder at once.

### Manual rating {#term-manual-rating}

A grade a person sets with a required reason, possibly without statements. It has no recommended limit.

### Maximum liability {#term-max-liability}

The cap on all the insurer's payments under a policy, usually a multiple of the premium.

### Non-qualifying loss (NQL) {#term-nql}

A threshold: a loss below it is not paid at all; one equal to it or above is paid in full, with nothing deducted for the threshold.

### Overdue notification {#term-noa}

The policyholder's report that a buyer has not paid an invoice beyond the maximum payment terms. It suspends the limit on the buyer.

### Policyholder {#term-policyholder}

Our client — the seller who insured their receivables.

### Policyholder tier {#term-policyholder-tier}

Bronze, silver, gold or platinum — by insured turnover and premium. A service class; it affects no decision.

### Possible match {#term-possible-match}

A company's or a person's name resembles a sanctions list entry. It waits for a credit analyst's review and blocks a limit approval until then.

### Rating band {#term-rating-band}

The family of a grade — A, B, C, D or "unrated". Authority for limit decisions is set per band.

### Rating proposal {#term-rating-proposal}

A grade an analyst proposed that waits for a colleague's or an authority level's approval.

### Rating scorecard {#term-scorecard}

Four pillars of assessment — general, equity and debt, profit and loss, payment behaviour — with sub-grades, weights and reasons, from which the system computes the weighted grade.

### Recommended grade {#term-recommended-grade}

The grade the model calculates, before a person confirms it.

### Recommended limit {#term-recommended-limit}

The limit the model calculates from the buyer's statements.

### Request withdrawal {#term-withdrawal}

The author of a request or the client gives it up; the request closes without a decision.

### Sales window {#term-sales-window}

The time sales has to hold a limit decision before it reaches the client. Not held — the decision goes by itself.

### Sanctions {#term-sanctions}

A sanctions flag on a country or a company means no cover; the database refuses new limits, submissions and policies for it.

### Submission {#term-submission}

A client's way from enquiry to policy: entity resolution, underwriting, commercial review, sales confirmation, the client's answer, binding.

### Turnover declaration {#term-turnover-declaration}

The policyholder's periodic report of insured sales per buyer and country; the basis of the premium.
