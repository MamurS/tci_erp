## When to use it

A claim is approved — time to pay; or an amount has been recovered from the buyer.

## Steps

1. {{ui:claims.tabs.money}} › payment: the amount may not exceed the approved indemnity.
2. Recovery: enter the amount received and the cost of recovering it.
3. The system splits it: costs first, the rest in proportion to the loss each side bore — the insurer the indemnity paid, the policyholder the rest of the debt (uncovered invoices, the retained share, the NQL, deductibles).

## Result

The split of each amount is stored as it was and never recalculated afterwards.

## Common mistakes

- **Expecting rounding to break the split.** The policyholder's share is the remainder, so the two shares always add up to the amount recovered.
