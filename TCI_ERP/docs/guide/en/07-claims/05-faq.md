**Why is the claim "partly approved" when I pressed "approve"?**
The status follows from the uncovered part of the debt: if some invoices are not covered, the claim is partly approved.

**Can I change the system's verdict?**
No, but you can add your own with a reason — yours becomes effective, and the system's stays on record.

**How do I remove an uploaded document?**
Your own file that is not registered yet can be removed; a registered one cannot.
