## When to use it

Every day.

## Steps

1. {{ui:nav.agenda}}: new claims, policyholders' answers to information requests, overdue notifications whose waiting period has run out.
2. {{ui:nav.claims}}: the claims queue by status.

{{shot:claims-queue}}

3. {{ui:nav.overdues}}: overdue accounts that may become claims.

## Result

Every claim moves: checked, information requested, or decided.

## Common mistakes

- **Losing track of notification dates.** An overdue notification filed late takes the cover away from the whole claim — check the dates.
