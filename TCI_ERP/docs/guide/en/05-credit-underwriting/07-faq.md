**Why was my decision "not recorded"?**
The amount in UZS is above your authority for the buyer's rating band. Send the decision for validation.

**Why is a request "Escalated" although I did not send it?**
The decision would have exceeded the group limit — the request was raised to be decided with the whole group in view.

**There is no exchange rate — what now?**
Without a UZS rate the amount cannot be compared with authority. The administrator enters rates.

**Where is the decision history on a buyer?**
On the request page and on the {{ui:entities.tabs.limits}} tab of the buyer's card: every decision is a row that never changes; a new decision supersedes the old one.
