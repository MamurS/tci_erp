title: Credit underwriting
roles: [credit_underwriter]
---

The credit underwriter makes the **credit decisions on limits** — approve, approve partly or decline — monitors active limits, revokes limits when the risk worsens, and watches groups and their limits. In "combined" mode they also rate and confirm grades.

A decision is made within one's {{term:authority|authority}} — an amount per {{term:rating-band|rating band}} of the buyer. Above it, the decision is sent for validation up the {{term:authority-level|authority levels}}, up to the {{term:committee|committee}}.
