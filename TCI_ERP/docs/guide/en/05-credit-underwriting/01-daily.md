## When to use it

Every day.

## Steps

1. {{ui:nav.agenda}}: requests to decide (addressed to you by name when your authority covers the amount), escalations, colleagues' validation steps, decisions on hold, limit reviews (≤ 30 days to go), a group over its limit, sanctions reviews, a country or sector downgrade.
2. {{ui:nav.limits}} › {{ui:limits.views.queue}}: each request shows its rating band and whether it is "within my authority".

{{shot:credit-queue}}

3. Open a request and decide it (see {{link:credit-decide}}).
4. {{ui:limits.views.monitoring}}: reviews, worsened grades, open overdue notifications, groups near their cap.

## Result

The queue is worked, no review is overdue.

## Common mistakes

- **Deciding a request you raised yourself.** The system will not let you — the four-eyes rule.
