{{ui:nav.limits}} is the credit desk's workplace. The views switch with the tabs under the title:

| View | What it shows |
|---|---|
| {{ui:limits.views.overview}} | The limit book: amounts by currency, rating band and country. |
| {{ui:limits.views.queue}} | Requests to decide: band, amount in UZS, "within my authority". |
| {{ui:limits.views.inforce}} | The register of active limits, with filters. |
| {{ui:limits.views.monitoring}} | Reviews ≤ 30 days and overdue, a grade worse than at the decision, open overdue notifications, groups near their cap. |
| {{ui:limits.views.ratings}} | The rating worklist, with the reason. |
| {{ui:limits.views.groups}} | Groups and their limits. |
| {{ui:limits.views.authority}} | Your authority by band. |
| {{ui:limits.views.auto}} | Auto decisions under ADT. |

The views depend on the role: sales and commercial underwriting see the queue and the register; a credit analyst sees the analysis views without the decision queue.
