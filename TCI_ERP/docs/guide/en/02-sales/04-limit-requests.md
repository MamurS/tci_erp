## When to use it

An existing policyholder needs a limit on a new buyer, or a larger limit on a known one.

## Steps

1. Open the policy or {{ui:nav.limits}} and press {{ui:limits.requestLimit}}.
2. Pick the buyer from the registry, enter the amount, currency, payment terms and the justification.
3. As you type the amount, the form tells you whether it is within the policy's {{term:dl|discretionary limit}}, above it and by how much.
4. Submit. If the buyer lacks data — a registration number, an address, an industry or recent statements — the request goes to {{term:information-gathering|information gathering}} with an information manager and comes back to the queue by itself.

## Result

The request is in the credit underwriting queue. You see the decision on the request page and in your Agenda.

## Common mistakes

- **Raising a second request on the same buyer under the same policy.** Only one request may be open per policy–buyer pair.
- **Expecting to decide it yourself.** Whoever raised a request never reviews or decides it — the {{term:four-eyes|four-eyes}} rule.
