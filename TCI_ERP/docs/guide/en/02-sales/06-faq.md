**Why does the client not see an approved limit?**
A decision reaches the client after the commercial stage and the sales window. Reductions and revocations reach them at once.

**Can I change a policy's terms?**
No, the commercial underwriter changes them. Sales confirms them at {{ui:requests.statuses.sales_confirmation}}.

**The client declined the offer. What next?**
The submission becomes {{ui:requests.statuses.declined}} and a notice appears in your Agenda — close it by hand once the file is closed.

**Why is a limit request "Gathering data"?**
The buyer lacks data needed for a decision. An information manager adds it, and the request returns to the queue by itself.
