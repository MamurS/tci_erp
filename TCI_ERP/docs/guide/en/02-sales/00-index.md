title: Sales
roles: [sales]
---

Sales takes a client from the first {{term:submission|submission}} to a bound policy: creates the submission, gathers the buyer package, confirms the terms, sends the client a {{term:commercial-offer|commercial offer}} and, once the client agrees, binds the submission into a policy. Sales also raises {{term:limit-request|credit limit requests}} and sees the register of active limits.

Sales **does not** make credit decisions and does not change policy terms — the underwriters do. A limit decision reaches the client by itself unless sales holds it before the {{term:sales-window|sales window}} runs out; reductions and revocations reach the client at once.
