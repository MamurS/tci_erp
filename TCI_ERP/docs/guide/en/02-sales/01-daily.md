## When to use it

Every morning and after every notice in the Agenda.

## Steps

1. Open {{ui:nav.agenda}} in the {{ui:nav.workspaces.sales.name}} workspace. Typical tasks: confirm a submission's terms (due when the sales window ends), the client accepted — bind the policy, the client declined, a declaration falls due, an offer expires soon.
2. Open {{ui:nav.requests}}: submissions in {{ui:requests.statuses.sales_confirmation}} are waiting for you. Check the terms and limits, prepare an offer and send it to the client (see {{link:sales-offers}}).
3. Bind submissions in {{ui:requests.statuses.accepted}} into policies (see {{link:sales-submissions}}).
4. An existing client needs a limit on a new buyer — {{ui:limits.requestLimit}} (see {{link:sales-limit-requests}}).
5. Check your clients' {{ui:nav.declarations}} and {{ui:nav.overdues}}.

## Result

The Agenda is empty or holds only tasks due later.

## Common mistakes

- **Waiting for the sales window to close by itself.** If something is wrong with the underwriters' decision, hold it before the window ends — after that the decision goes to the client.
- **Looking for a limit decision button.** Sales makes no decisions; your work is the submission and the conversation with the client.
