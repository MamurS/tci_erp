## When to use it

The submission has reached commercial review, sales confirmation or the client, and the client needs a formal offer.

## Steps

1. Open the submission and prepare an offer — in Russian, English or Uzbek. The language is chosen when the offer is prepared and does not change.
2. Choose the parameters: the cover, the validity, whether to include the TCI brochure and a short note (up to 600 characters). The document's texts are approved and not edited; the data — terms, premium, limits per buyer, the insurer's particulars — the system fills in from the submission at every save.
3. Check the preview on the right. A buyer's limit shows as "approved" (an effective decision), "declined" or "indicative" (the amount requested).
4. Publish: {{ui:offers.edit.saveAndSend}}. Sales sends to the client at sales confirmation; before that the offer can be prepared, and the screen says what is missing.
5. The client opens the offer in the portal — sales is told. The client accepts, asks for changes or declines.

## Result

Accepting the offer in the portal is the client's acceptance of the submission.

## Common mistakes

- **Expecting an expired offer to be accepted.** The validity is binding: after it the offer cannot be accepted, only a new one requested.
- **Looking for the buyer's grade in the offer.** An offer never shows a grade, the recommended limit, an authority or an internal comment.
