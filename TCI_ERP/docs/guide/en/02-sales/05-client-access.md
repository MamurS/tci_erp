## When to use it

The policyholder wants to file declarations, limit requests and overdue notifications and answer offers in the portal themselves.

## Steps

1. Open the policyholder's card, tab {{ui:entities.tabs.access}}.
2. Press {{ui:provisioning.inviteClient}} and enter the email and the name of the client's employee.
3. Hand the temporary password to the client safely. They change it at the first sign-in.

## Result

The client's user sees only their own policies, limits, declarations, premium, overdue accounts, claims and offers.

## Common mistakes

- **The button is unavailable, "the provisioning service is unavailable".** The user-provisioning service is not hosted yet; until it is, the administrator creates client users by hand.
- **Giving a client a staff login.** The client role is never combined with any other role.
